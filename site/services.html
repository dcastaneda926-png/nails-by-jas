Good to know

Before you come

Take off any old polish if you can, skip cutting your cuticles, and come with clean dry hands.

How long it takes

Plan for the full time slot. Detailed art can run longer, and I'd rather not rush the finish.

What's included

Shaping, cuticle care, your color or design, and a top coat. Removal of my own work is always free.

How art pricing works

The base service covers your shape, prep, and a single color or a simple french. Anything beyond that gets added per nail, so a couple of accent nails costs a couple of accent nails — not a whole-set upcharge.

If you're not sure what your design would come to, send me the photo on Instagram before you book and I'll give you a number.

Not sure what to book?

Start with the full set if you want length, and the gel manicure if you like your natural nails and just want color. If you're between the two, book the gel manicure and we can talk it through in the chair.
