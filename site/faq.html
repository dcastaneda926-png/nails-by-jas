Questions

everything people usually ask ♡

How long will my nails last?

A gel manicure holds up about two weeks. Extensions and builder gel go two to three weeks before they need a fill, though it depends a lot on how hard you are on your hands. If something lifts in the first three days, tell me and I'll fix it free.

I've never had extensions. Will they wreck my nails?

Not if they come off properly. Almost all the damage people describe comes from picking or prying them off. Come in for a soak-off and your natural nail underneath will be fine.

Can I bring a picture of what I want?

Please do — it's the fastest way for me to understand what you're after. I'll tell you honestly whether it works on your nail shape and length, and what I'd adjust so it wears better.

Do you take walk-ins?

No, everything is by appointment. It's a one-chair setup, so booking ahead is the only way I can give you the full time slot.

Can you remove work from another salon?

Yes. Book the removal add-on along with whatever you're getting done and I'll take care of it in the same visit.

How do I make a set last longer?

Cuticle oil daily, gloves for dishes and cleaning, and don't use your nails as tools. Those three things make more difference than anything I can do in the chair.

How do I pay?

Card, Apple Pay, Venmo, or cash. The deposit is handled online when you book, and the rest is due at the appointment.
